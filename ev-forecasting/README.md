# EV spare parts forecasting

Group project in Programming for Management Studies at TUM Campus Heilbronn (group of five). I led the forecasting part.

The question: can a forecast with low statistical error still create high inventory cost? Across 24 parts and 78 weeks, the most accurate of five methods cost about 8 times more on the test weeks than a less accurate forecast with a safety buffer, because running out of a part costs far more than holding one extra.

Methods are limited to what the course taught: pandas, NumPy and scikit-learn linear regression.
